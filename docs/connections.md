# Pin-by-pin connections

Every pin of every active device, the net it sits on, and what else is on
that net. Generated by `hardware/make_connections.py` from the schematic
files, so it cannot drift from the design. Passive two-pin parts are listed
in the net summary at the bottom rather than getting a table each.

Net names follow the design's conventions exactly: `CHIP_ENABLE`, `BOOT`,
`LED_DATA`, `UART_RX`/`UART_TX` (named from the MCU's point of view),
`VBUS`, `VBAT`, `VBAT_SW`, `FT_3V3`, `+3.3V`, `GND`.

---
