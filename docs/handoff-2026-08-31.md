# Twinkling Butterfly — Project Handoff
### Custom PCB, KiCad 10.0.5, hierarchical schematic | Handoff date: August 31, 2026

## What this project is

A butterfly-shaped ornament with 6 addressable RGB LEDs (WS2812B) that twinkle at night. Powered by two LIR2032 rechargeable coin cells in parallel (~80 mAh), charged over USB-C, reprogrammable over the same USB-C port via an onboard USB-to-UART bridge with auto-program transistors (no BOOT/RESET buttons — the only physical control is a power slide switch). Everything hand-soldered: all passives 1206 or larger, leaded or large-pad packages everywhere except the QFN-32 MCU, SSOP-20 bridge, SOT-23 parts, and the USB-C connector.

## Architecture in one paragraph

USB-C VBUS feeds an MCP73831 charger (≈42 mA via 24 kΩ PROG) into the battery pair, and feeds the FT231XS bridge's VCC directly. The battery runs through slide switch SW1 into an AP2112K-3.3 LDO for the ESP32-C3FH4 (bare chip, 4 MB internal flash, 40 MHz crystal). The FT231XS converts USB to UART on GPIO20/21 and its DTR#/RTS# drive two cross-coupled MMBT2222A transistors that pull CHIP_EN and GPIO9 for esptool's one-click flashing. GPIO4 drives the WS2812B chain (powered from the switched battery rail, not the LDO). Native USB (GPIO18/19) goes to test pads as a backup flashing path. The charger stays wired to the battery so charging works with SW1 off; the ESP32 must be ON to flash.

## Design decisions already settled (do not relitigate without cause)

1. Bare ESP32-C3FH4 over modules — user explicitly banned modules and is comfortable with QFN and complex routing.
2. FT231XS replaced CH340C because DigiKey no longer stocks CH340C as a bare chip. Bonus: FT231XS runs from 5 V VBUS with internal 3.3 V I/O regulation, which eliminated the second LDO entirely.
3. USB4110-GF-A (all-SMD) replaced USB4105 (hybrid THT) at user request for surface-mount.
4. Two LIR2032 in parallel (NOT series) — keeps single-cell charging, 3.0–4.2 V rail, ~80 mAh, ~40–60 mA continuous capability. Same brand/batch, replaced as a pair, holders labeled LIR ONLY (CR2032 here = fire risk).
5. WS2812B ×6 (trimmed from 8: idle draw ~0.8 mA each matters on coin cells). Firmware caps brightness ~10%, max 3 pixels lit at once, light sleep between frames. Runtime ≈ 3–4 h/charge.
6. All passives 1206; crystal in 5032 4-pad package for hand-solderability.
7. Amber-only single-color LEDs and the pouch-cell/XIAO variants were earlier iterations (v1/v2) and are superseded.

## Net naming conventions (exact spellings matter)

- `CHIP_ENABLE` — ESP pin 7 node (pin + R1 10k-to-3V3 + C10 1 µF + label). Q1 collector pulls it.
- `BOOT` — GPIO9 (pin 15) node (pin + R4 10k-to-3V3 + label, deliberately NO cap). Q2 collector pulls it.
- `LED_DATA` — GPIO4 (pin 9) → 470 Ω → first WS2812B DIN.
- `UART_RX` / `UART_TX` — named from the MCU's point of view. UART_TX (ESP pin 28) → FT231XS RXD; UART_RX (ESP pin 27) ← FT231XS TXD. Each has a 470 Ω series resistor on the bridge side.
- `USB_DATA+` / `USB_DATA−` — GPIO19/18 to test pads on the MCU sheet (backup native-USB flashing).
- `VBUS` (5 V from USB), `+3.3V` (battery-fed LDO output), `VBAT_SW` (switched battery rail feeding LDO and LED chain), GND. Power symbols are global; only signals use hierarchical labels/sheet pins.

# Upgrade path: proper 5 V NeoPixel drive for maximum brightness

WS2812Bs are noticeably brighter and truer-colored at 5 V — blue and green dim badly below ~3.5 V supply, which is why colors shift as a LiPo discharges. Running them at 5 V "properly" needs two additions, because two separate specs break:

**Problem 1 — supply.** The battery gives 3.0–4.2 V, so a boost converter must create the 5 V LED rail.
- **U7 — MCP1640T-I/OT** boost converter, SOT-23-6, hand-solderable — https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=MCP1640T-I%2FOT
- **L1 — 4.7 µH power inductor**, ~1 A sat, two-pad shielded (e.g., Bourns SRN4018 series) — https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=SRN4018-4R7M
- **C — 10 µF 1206 in, 2× 10 µF 1206 out** (same CL31A106KAHNNNE)
- **Feedback divider:** VOUT = 1.21 V × (1 + R_top/R_bot). Use **R_top = 470 kΩ, R_bot = 150 kΩ** → 5.00 V. 1206: https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=470k%201206%201%25 and https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=150k%201206%201%25
- Wiring: VIN + EN ← VBAT_SW (EN tied to VIN so SW1 still kills everything); SW pin → inductor → ... per the datasheet's standard boost topology (verify pin roles against the MCP1640 datasheet when drawing; SOT-23-6 is SW, GND, FB, EN, VIN, VOUT). The 5 V output becomes the new LED rail `+5V_LED`; move the 470 µF and the per-pixel 0.1 µFs onto it.

**Problem 2 — logic level.** A WS2812B at 5 V requires data-high ≥ 0.7 × VDD = 3.5 V, and the ESP32's 3.3 V no longer qualifies; pixels glitch or ignore data. The canonical fix:
- **U8 — SN74AHCT1G125DBVR**, single-gate buffer, SOT-23-5 — https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=SN74AHCT1G125DBVR
- The AHCT family has TTL input thresholds (VIH = 2.0 V), so it reads 3.3 V cleanly while its output swings the full 5 V.
- Wiring: pin 5 VCC → `+5V_LED` with 0.1 µF; pin 3 GND; pin 2 A ← `LED_DATA` from GPIO4; pin 1 OE̅ → GND (always enabled); pin 4 Y → 470 Ω → first pixel DIN. The 470 Ω moves to the 5 V side of the buffer.

**The honest physics caveat:** boosting doubles the current story. Six pixels at even 40% brightness ≈ 290 mA at 5 V ≈ 450+ mA drawn from the battery through the boost. Two LIR2032s cannot do that — they'll sag and brown out the board. So this upgrade only makes sense paired with the 500 mAh pouch cell (swap R_PROG to 2 kΩ for ~500 mA charging, delete the coin holders, add JST S2B-PH-K-S — https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=S2B-PH-K-S ). On the coin pair, skip the boost entirely and keep the 3.0–4.2 V direct-drive design; capped at 10% brightness the color shift is barely visible. A good compromise: lay out the boost + buffer + JST footprints on the PCB but leave them unpopulated (DNP) on coin-cell builds — one board, both personalities.
